Field Mapping: Evaluation & Offered

Which value goes where: screen, database, Excel and email. Updated 19 Oct 2026.

When HR moves a candidate forward, a popup opens. On Confirm, the program reads the candidate from the database, builds the Excel form or the email, saves the popup values, then moves the stage. If the Excel or the email fails, the stage does not move. Emails are saved as an Outlook draft with "To" left empty.

4 Typed or picked on the screen 14 Taken from the database or filled automatically (not on the screen) [applicants].[position] = table dbo.applicants, column position

Hover over (or tap) any number, field or table row to light up the same field on the screen, in the database, in Excel and in the email.

Table relations

All candidate data for both stages lives in one row of [applicants]. The other tables link to it by key.

  • [jobs] 1 → N [applicants] on job_id: the job posting gives the default Role ([jobs].[title]).
  • [requests] 1 → N [applicants] on request_id: picking a hiring request copies its Position, Company, Department and Section onto the candidate.
  • [users] 1 → N [applicants] on owner_email = email: the recruiter who owns the card.
  • [users] 1 → N [user_signatures] on user_id: the logged-in user's signature for each email type.
  • [applicants] 1 → N [stage_history] on application_id: one row each time the stage moves.

Things to watch

  • The offer email takes Expected Salary 6 from [applicants].[expect_salary] (scraped), but the report uses [applicants].[expect_salary_edit] (edited by HR). The two can differ.
  • Reopening the Offer popup does not bring back what was typed before (Supervisor, Buddy, Start date, comments).
  • The Offer details (Supervisor, Buddy, Start date, salaries) are saved but are not in the Excel report.
  • To change Position, Department, Section or Role for an offer, move the candidate back to Evaluation and redo the form.
